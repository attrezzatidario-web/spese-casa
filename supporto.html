Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?
Enter your email: you will receive a code to type into the app. No password needed.

How do I share the home with my family?
With Premium: Other → Account → Invite. Whoever receives the link or the code joins your home and sees the same data.

How do I cancel Premium?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I turn on artificial intelligence?
Other → Artificial intelligence: paste a free Google Gemini key created on Google AI Studio.

I don't receive notifications.
Check Other → Notifications and that notifications for Spese Casa are allowed in your phone settings.

How do I delete my account?
You will find all the instructions on the Delete your account page.
